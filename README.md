# Rust Learning Notes

This repository contains my personal notes and summaries while learning Rust.

The goal of these notes is not to provide a complete Rust reference, but to record my understanding, mistakes, and discoveries throughout the learning process.

Topics covered include:

* Ownership and Borrowing
* Lifetimes
* Rc and Arc
* Traits and Generics
* Iterators
* Closuress
* Async/Await
* Futures
* Send and Sync
* Result and Option
* and more

Many notes include common misunderstandings I had and the thought process that helped me understand the concepts.

I hope these notes are useful not only for my future self, but also for other Rust learners who may encounter similar questions.

## Disclaimer

These notes reflect my current understanding at the time they were written. Some explanations may be simplified, incomplete, or updated as my knowledge grows.
